World transformation:
- Translation (x,y,z) --> (x+0x, y+0y, z+0z)
- Rotation (x, y, z) --> (dx*x, dy*y, dz*z)
- Scale (x, y, z) --> (x, y) = x(1,0) +y(0,1)
			(1,0)-->(0,1)
			(0,1)-->(-1,0)
			(x,y)-->x(0,1)+y(-1,0) -->(-y,x)
			x(cos0, sin0) +y(-sin0, cos0)-->(xcos0-ysin0, xsin0+ycos0)
			(x,y)	(cos0 sin0) <=(1,0)
				(-sin0 cos0)<=(0,1)
